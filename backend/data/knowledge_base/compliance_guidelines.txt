Export compliance for Ceylon tea. Each destination enforces maximum pesticide residue limits (MRL) and maximum moisture content; Japan and the EU (Germany) apply the strictest MRLs. Required certifications vary: ISO 22000 food safety is near-universal, Halal is mandatory for Middle East markets, and Organic / JAS / Rainforest Alliance are required for premium European and Japanese buyers. A shipment failing any mandatory certification or exceeding an MRL/moisture limit must not be exported to that market.
