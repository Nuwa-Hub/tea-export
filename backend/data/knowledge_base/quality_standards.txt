Grades and quality. Common Ceylon grades from finest to coarse include BOPF (Broken Orange Pekoe Fannings), BOP (Broken Orange Pekoe) and OP (Orange Pekoe). Premium markets (Japan, China, Germany) require finer BOPF-grade leaf with low moisture; bulk markets accept OP/BOP. Matching grade to the destination's minimum requirement is a precondition for export readiness.
